About TRUST Corporation

Built On Reliability.
Driven By Results.

PT. Trust Eadyra Line (TRUST Corporation) is an integrated maritime logistics and offshore support company established in 2010 in Jakarta, Indonesia. Dedicated to service excellence, we deliver large-scale ocean bulk cargo transportation, a reliable fleet of tugboats & 300ft barges, and international-standard offshore well testing support.

Guided by our motto "Innovate & Transform to Deliver", we combine seasoned maritime expertise with world-class safety certifications (BKI, ABS, ISO, IMO ISM Code). Through strategic alliances with industry leaders Schlumberger and Svitzer, TRUST is committed to keeping national energy and industrial supply chains moving forward seamlessly.
