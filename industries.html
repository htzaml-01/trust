SECTORS & EXPERTISE

Empowering Key Industries Across the Indonesian Archipelago

From heavy mining concessions and offshore oilfield fields to forestry timber transport and coastal infrastructure, TRUST Corporation delivers specialized marine logistics, modern tug & barge fleets, and uncompromising safety standards.

Coal, Nickel Ore & Mineral Barging

High-capacity bulk transportation of thermal coal, nickel ore, iron ore, and industrial limestone across domestic inter-island routes and offshore transshipment anchorages. Operating high-side 300ft deck cargo barges capable of moving up to 10,000 DWT per shipment.

Oil, Gas & Offshore Marine Support

Comprehensive offshore marine operations in strategic alliance with Schlumberger (SLB). Providing DST offshore well testing barges, wireline platforms, cementing units, AHTS, supply vessels, and crew transfer boats for upstream exploration and production.

Plantation Logs & Pulpwood Carrier

Specialized coastal and river barging for industrial plantation timber (acacia, gemelina, mixed hardwood) serving Indonesia's largest pulp and paper producers (APRIL, RAPP, TEL) with strict on-time delivery.

Heavy Machinery & EPC Project Cargo

Roll-on/Roll-off (RoRo) barging and heavy-lift logistics for excavators, dump trucks, mining crushers, power plant transformers, and prefabricated refinery modules requiring precise ballasting and load distribution.

Shore Base Logistics & Port Terminals

Full-service shore base facilities, open stockpile management, berthing facilities, and dedicated bonded warehousing designed for marine equipment staging, fuel bunkering, and pipe storage.

Marine Salvage & Emergency Response

Certified salvage assistance and emergency rescue operations in partnership with Svitzer Salvage, BV. Rapid deployment for grounded vessels, towage assistance, wreck removal, and offshore containment across Southeast Asia.
