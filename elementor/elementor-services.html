Integrated Marine Logistics Services

Integrated Ocean Bulk Cargo & Offshore Engineering Solutions

Providing dedicated tugboat fleets, ocean bulk barges, DST offshore well testing facilities, and agency services with international standards across the Indonesian archipelago.

Bulk Cargo & Ocean Barging

Large-scale ocean bulk cargo transportation for coal, industrial forestry timber (pulp & paper), nickel, and raw minerals with modern tugboats and 300ft barges certified by BKI & ABS.

Offshore Well Testing & DST

Specialized offshore DST Well Testing Barges, Work Barges, cementing, coiled-tubing, and well intervention support delivered through an integrated partnership with Schlumberger.

Offshore Support Fleet (AHTS)

Operation of Anchor Handling Tug Supply (AHTS), Platform Supply Vessels, Utility Vessels, and fast Crew Boats adhering to rigorous oil & gas safety standards for offshore exploration.

Integrated Ship Management

Comprehensive vessel technical management, planned dry-docking maintenance, classification compliance (BKI & ABS), IMO ISM Code safety standards, and STCW-certified marine crew.

Shipping Agency & Port Handling

Integrated harbor agency operations, port authority clearances, bulk stevedoring, freight forwarding, and rapid offshore transshipment facilities.

Shore Base & Marine Salvage

Dedicated shore base logistics facilities, stockpile management, and regional emergency maritime assistance (salvage operations) in strategic alliance with Svitzer (Maersk Group).
