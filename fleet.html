Armada & Marine Equipment Details

Each vessel is outfitted with marine telemetry, modern navigation electronics, and heavy-duty structural reinforcements to meet demanding industrial and offshore charter standards.

Tugboat Class BKI

TRITON 501 & TRUST OCEAN Series

High-bollard pull ocean-going tugboat series designed for open-sea long-distance towing, STS tanker assistance, and heavy barge escort across Indonesian archipelagic waters.

  • Engine Horsepower: 2,000 – 3,200 BHP (Twin Screw)
  • Main Engines: Yanmar / Mitsubishi Marine Diesel
  • Bollard Pull: 28 – 45 Metric Tons
  • Navigation & Comms: GMDSS A1+A2+A3, AIS Class A
  • Primary Role: Ocean Towage & STS Transshipment
Deck Cargo Barge Class BKI

TRUST MEGA 333 & 300 Series

Heavy-duty flat top deck cargo barge with heavy reinforced sideboards, engineered for high-volume coal, nickel ore, bauxite transshipment, and heavy project machinery transport.

  • Dimensions: 300 ft x 80 ft x 18 ft (91.4m x 24.4m)
  • Deadweight (DWT): 8,000 – 10,000 Metric Tons
  • Deck Load Rating: 8.0 – 10.0 Ton / m²
  • Sideboard Height: 12 – 14 ft Heavy Steel Coaming
  • Cargo Compatibility: Coal, Nickel Ore, Heavy EPC Equipment
Offshore Work Barge Class ABS / BKI

TRUST OFFSHORE 01 / DST Barge

Multi-deck offshore accommodation and well-intervention work barge, operated in strategic coordination with Schlumberger (SLB) and Pertamina for deepwell DST testing and rig support.

  • Accommodation: 60 – 120 POB Air-Conditioned Quarters
  • Well-Testing Roles: DST, Wireline, Coiled Tubing
  • Mooring System: 4-Point / 8-Point Heavy Anchor Winches
  • Deck Crane: Pedestal Heavy-Lift Offshore Crane
  • Safety Compliance: SKT Migas, ISM & ISPS Certified
AHTS & Rig Support Class BKI

TRUST ENTERPRISE AHTS & Support

Anchor Handling Tug Supply vessel engineered for offshore platform resupply, exploratory drilling rig moves, external firefighting (FiFi), and open-sea emergency response.

  • BHP Rating: 5,150 – 6,500 BHP
  • Clear Deck Space: 350 m² Timber Deck
  • Towing / Anchor Winch: 150 MT Line Pull Double Drum
  • Tank Capacities: Fuel Oil, Drill Water, Liquid Mud
  • Firefighting: FiFi 1 Water Monitors with Deluge System
Marine Engineering Class BKI

TRUST DREDGE & Engineering Support

Specialized coastal engineering and dredging support vessel used for river mouth navigation channel deepening, jetty construction, and offshore pipeline trench backfilling.

  • Deck Equipment: Heavy Crawler Crane & Clamshell
  • Positioning: Hydraulic Spud Legs & Winches
  • Max Dredging Depth: Up to 22 Meters
  • Operations: Jetty Construction & Stockpile Dredging
  • Service Area: Kalimantan, Sumatra & Sulawesi
Hydrographic & Crew Class ABS

TRUST SURVEY & Rapid Dispatch

High-speed oceanographic survey and rapid offshore personnel transfer vessel, outfitted with helideck landing capabilities and multi-beam bathymetric seabed mapping sensors.

  • Service Speed: 18 – 22 Knots High-Speed Transit
  • Aviation Facility: Helideck Rated for Medium Helicopters
  • Survey Suite: Multi-Beam Echo Sounder & Side Scan Sonar
  • Passenger Capacity: 36 Offshore Technical Personnel
  • Mission Focus: Seabed Survey & Rig Crew Dispatch
