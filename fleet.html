OUR OPERATIONAL FLEET

Modern Maritime Fleet & Assets

High-specification tugboats, heavy-duty deck cargo barges, and offshore work barges delivering zero-downtime reliability across Indonesian waters.

Bulk Cargo & Ocean Barging

Large-scale ocean bulk cargo transportation for coal, industrial forestry timber (pulp & paper), nickel, and raw minerals with modern tugboats and 300ft barges certified by BKI & ABS.

Offshore Well Testing & DST

Specialized offshore DST Well Testing Barges, Work Barges, cementing, coiled-tubing, and well intervention support delivered through an integrated partnership with Schlumberger.

Offshore Support Fleet (AHTS)

Operation of Anchor Handling Tug Supply (AHTS), Platform Supply Vessels, Utility Vessels, and fast Crew Boats adhering to rigorous oil & gas safety standards for offshore exploration.

Heavy-Duty Deck Cargo Barges

Flat top 300ft and 330ft deck cargo barges with high sideboards engineered for high deck load ratings (8–10 T/m²) carrying heavy machinery, transformers, and mining equipment.

Ocean Towage & Tugboat Series

Twin-screw ocean-going tugboats from 2,000 to 3,200 BHP delivering continuous bollard pull for coastal barge towing, ship-to-ship (STS) escort, and emergency salvage response.

Specialized Survey & Rapid Transfer

High-speed oceanographic bathymetry and rapid offshore crew dispatch vessels featuring certified aviation helideck and advanced multi-beam echo sounder sonar sensors.

PT. Trust Eadyra Line delivers integrated marine logistics, tug & barge chartering, bulk cargo transshipment, offshore DST well testing support, and certified ship management across Indonesia and international waters.
